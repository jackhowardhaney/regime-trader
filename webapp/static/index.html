Why does Account Value differ from Capital Deployed?

Account Value (Equity) is your true net worth — cash + current market value of all open positions. This started at $10k.
Capital Deployed is the gross cost of all open positions combined. Alpaca paper trading provides 2:1 overnight margin, meaning your $10k equity can control up to $20k in positions — they lend you the other half.
So the gap isn't losses — it's leverage. Your P&L risk is still capped to your equity. If positions move against you, losses come out of your $99k, not the borrowed margin.
